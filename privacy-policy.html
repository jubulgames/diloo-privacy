This Privacy Policy explains what data Jubul Games ("we", "us", "our") collects when you play Diloo, a free Turkish-learning game, why we collect it, who we share it with, and how you can delete it. If you don't agree with this policy, please don't use Diloo.

Want to delete your account and data? Open Diloo and go to Settings → Delete Account, or email us with your Player ID. Full steps: Delete your account and data.

Summary of key points

  • Who it's for: players 13 and older. We don't ask for or store your age.
  • No login needed: Diloo creates an anonymous account automatically. There is no email, password or social login.
  • What we collect: your display name, your game progress, gameplay analytics, crash reports, and device and advertising data used by our ad partners.
  • What we don't collect: age, GPS or precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera, or payment details. There is no text or voice chat.
  • Ads: Diloo is free and shows ads. You can change your ad choices any time in Settings → Ad Preferences.
  • Your rights: you can ask to access, correct or delete your data by emailing us. We reply within 30 days.

1. Who Diloo is for

In shortDiloo is for players aged 13 and over. We don't collect age.

Diloo is intended for players 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We don't ask for, collect or store anyone's age or date of birth.

The age at which you can agree to the use of your data yourself is different in different countries (in the EU, for example, it is between 13 and 16). If you are under that age where you live, please use Diloo only with your parent's or guardian's permission.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used Diloo, contact us at diloo.support@protonmail.com and we will delete their data (see Section 8).

2. What information we collect

In shortWe collect the display name you choose, your game progress, how the game is used, crash data, and device and advertising data needed to show ads.

Information you give us

  • Display name. You choose it during the tutorial; a nickname is fine. Other players can see it in multiplayer lobbies.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message.

Your anonymous account and saved progress

  • Anonymous account. Diloo signs you in automatically with Unity Authentication, without any email, password, or Google or Apple login. Your Player ID is shown in Settings once you are signed in.
  • Saved progress (stored with Unity Cloud Save): display name, level and XP, coins and gems, selected and owned avatars, unlocked game modes, stars and scores per level, gameplay statistics, tutorial progress, an ads setting, and your Daily Word results.
  • Live game content. Diloo downloads Daily Word content through Unity Remote Config. To do this, the app sends your app's identifiers and basic device information.

Information collected automatically

  • Gameplay analytics (Unity Analytics): events such as tutorial progress, level starts and results, Daily Word play, multiplayer activity and ad interactions. Unity Analytics also collects your IP address, an installation ID, your Player ID, and device and app information. Our custom analytics events never include your display name.
  • Crash and performance data (Unity Diagnostics): crash reports, error logs and stability and performance data, linked to an installation ID, together with device model, operating system and app version.
  • Multiplayer data (Photon): when you join a multiplayer lobby, Photon processes your IP address and a user or session ID to connect you, and passes your display name to the other players in that lobby. Diloo has no text or voice chat.
  • Advertising data (our ad partners, see Section 4): your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and network information, approximate location worked out from your IP address, and information about the ads you see and tap. Your ad privacy choices are stored on your device by Google's consent tool.

What we do not collect

We don't collect your age or date of birth, precise (GPS) location, contacts, photos or files, microphone or camera data, or payment information. Diloo has no in-app purchases. We don't process sensitive personal information, and we don't use cookies on this page.

4. Who we share information with

In shortWe share data with the service providers that run Diloo and with our ad partners. We don't sell your data for money.
  • Unity Technologies: Authentication, Cloud Save, Remote Config, Analytics, Diagnostics, Unity Ads and LevelPlay ad mediation. Unity Game Player & App User Privacy Policy
  • ironSource (a Unity company), ad network through LevelPlay. Unity / ironSource Privacy Policy
  • Google: AdMob ads and the User Messaging Platform consent form. Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Photon (Exit Games): multiplayer networking, acting on our behalf. Photon Privacy Policy
  • Other players see your display name in multiplayer lobbies.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it or to protect the rights and safety of players or others.
  • Business transfers: if Diloo or Jubul Games is merged, sold or transferred, your data may move to the new owner, who must follow this policy.

Ad partners use the data they collect under their own privacy policies (linked above), including to measure and personalize ads where you allow it.

"Sale" and "sharing" under US state privacy laws. We don't sell your data for money. However, letting ad partners collect your advertising ID and device data to show personalized ads may count as "selling" or "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under California and other US state laws. You can opt out in Settings → Ad Preferences, or by deleting your advertising ID on your device (Android: Settings → Google → Ads). We don't knowingly sell or share data of anyone under 16.

5. Your ad choices

In shortYou decide how ads use your data, and you can change your mind at any time.
  • When you first open Diloo, Google's consent form is shown where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) and in US states with privacy laws. Diloo only loads ads after this step, and only if your choices allow ads.
  • You can review or change your choices at any time in Settings → Ad Preferences.
  • If you turn personalization off, you may still see ads, but they won't be based on your data.

6. International transfers

In shortOur providers may process your data in countries other than yours.

Our service providers and ad partners process data in the United States, the European Union and other countries, which may be outside Türkiye, the EEA or the UK. Where available, we rely on the safeguards these providers offer for international transfers, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

7. How long we keep information

In shortWe keep your game account and progress until you ask us to delete them. Other data is deleted automatically after set periods.
  • Anonymous account and saved progress: until you delete your account or ask us to delete it.
  • Analytics: raw event data is deleted automatically after 13 months. Summary metrics and the analytics player profile are kept until you ask for deletion.
  • Crash and performance data: 90 days.
  • Multiplayer (Photon): processed while you are in a session and kept by Photon according to its own policy.
  • Support emails: 12 months after your request is resolved, then deleted.
  • Ad partners: according to their own privacy policies (see Section 4).

8. Delete your Diloo account and data

In shortYou can delete your account in the app, or ask us by email, even if you have uninstalled Diloo.

Diloo, published by Jubul Games, lets you delete your account and its data in two ways:

Option A: in the app

  • Open Diloo, go to Settings → Delete Account, and confirm.

Option B: by email (no app needed)

  • Email diloo.support@protonmail.com with the subject "Delete my Diloo account".
  • Include your Player ID (shown in Diloo's Settings). Because accounts are anonymous, the Player ID is how we find your data. If you no longer have it, tell us your display name and roughly when you started playing, and we'll do our best.
  • We confirm by email once your deletion is complete, within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your anonymous Unity account (Player ID) and all your saved progress: display name, level, XP, coins, gems, avatars, unlocks, scores, statistics, tutorial progress, settings and Daily Word results.
  • Your Unity Analytics data.
  • Crash and performance data, where it can be linked to you. Otherwise it is deleted automatically after 90 days.

Deletion is permanent and all your progress will be lost. Uninstalling the app does not delete your cloud data, so please use one of the options above. Data that ad partners hold under their own policies isn't covered by our deletion, but you can delete your advertising ID on your device and use the partners' links in Section 4.

9. Your privacy rights

In shortYou can access, correct or delete your data and control how it's used. Just email us.

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR, Türkiye's KVKK and US state privacy laws give you these rights:

  • Access: get a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correct: fix inaccurate data. You can also change your display name in the game.
  • Delete: have your account and data deleted (see Section 8).
  • Object or restrict: object to or limit how we use your data, including for analytics.
  • Withdraw consent and change ad choices: at any time in Settings → Ad Preferences. This doesn't affect anything done before you changed it.
  • Opt out of "sale" or "sharing" for targeted ads: in Settings → Ad Preferences.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority, for example your EU authority, the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), or Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK Kurumu).

How to ask: email diloo.support@protonmail.com, say which right you want to use, and include your Player ID so we can find your data and confirm it's yours. We reply within 30 days. We won't treat you differently for using your rights. If we can't fully help, we'll explain why, and you can reply to ask us to look at it again.

10. How we keep information safe

In shortWe use reasonable technical and organizational measures, but no system is 100% secure.

Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) to our service providers, and access to our service dashboards is limited to the Jubul Games team. No transmission over the internet or storage system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and will act promptly if a problem occurs.

11. Third-party links and ads

Ads in Diloo may link to other apps or websites that we don't control. This policy doesn't cover them, so please read their privacy policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Diloo or the law changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top, and for important changes we'll also let you know in the game or on our store listing.
